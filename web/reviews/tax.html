Tax to treasury

A report on one of the platform's own hooks, written from its code.

A share of every buy and every sell goes to a wallet the launcher chooses. The launcher sets a buy tax, a sell tax and a ceiling at launch. The owner of the tax can move both taxes between zero and the ceiling.

Its code is programs/hook_tax/src/lib.rs in the package.

    What it does

    On every buy the hook asks for the buy tax. On every sell it asks for the sell tax. The pool takes the tax in the quote token, SOL on this site, and pays it to the coin's treasury.

    The launcher creates the coin's tax settings before the pool exists. They hold the buy tax, the sell tax, the ceiling, the owner, and the wallet that created them.

    Who controls the program

    This is one of the platform's own hooks. Its upgrade authority is a key of Programmable that the platform whitelists, so the pool takes it without approval.

    Programmable's key can still upgrade it. An upgrade could change anything this report says. Programmable answers for it as it answers for the pool program itself.

    Its manifest flags are BEFORE_SWAP, ON_POOL_CREATE, TAKE_HOOK_FEE, CALL_ON_BUY, CALL_ON_SELL.

    What it can take, and where it goes

    A buy pays the buy tax. A sell pays the sell tax. Each tax is at most the ceiling.

    The ceiling is fixed when the settings are created. The program refuses a ceiling above 10%. The pool also cuts any hook fee to the platform's cap, now 10%.

    The tax goes to the pool's hook treasury. It is the wrapped SOL account of a wallet the launcher chooses at launch. The pool fixes it when the pool is created, and the hook cannot send the tax anywhere else.

    The hook holds no funds of its own.

    Can it refuse a trade

    No. Its answer before a trade only names the tax. It refuses no buy and no sell because of who trades, when or how much.

    A trade fails only for reasons any trade meets, such as wrong accounts or slippage. The hook never runs when liquidity is added or removed.

    What can change after launch, and who changes it

    InstructionWhoWhat it does
    set_taxthe owner of the taxSets the buy tax and the sell tax, each anywhere from zero up to the ceiling. It can raise them up to the ceiling.
    set_ownerthe owner of the taxHands the tax to another wallet, or renounces it. After a renounce the taxes can never change again.
    init_pool_configthe launcher, onceCreates the settings before the pool exists. On this site the launcher is also the owner.
    init_manifestthe upgrade authority, onceWrites the manifest and the list of extra accounts.
    on_pool_create, before_swapthe pool onlyThe calls above. They change nothing.

    Nothing else can change. The ceiling has no instruction that changes it. The treasury belongs to the pool, not to the hook. The coin's page shows both taxes, the ceiling and the owner, or that the owner renounced.

    The owner raising a tax up to the ceiling is within the review rules. The ceiling is fixed at launch and shown on the coin's page, and this report names who can change the tax.

    Its manifest and its account list

    init_manifest writes both once. It needs the program's upgrade authority, and an account that exists cannot be created again. No instruction rewrites either of them. The list names one account, the coin's tax settings, which the pool passes read only.

    Programs it calls

    The system program, to create its accounts at setup. No other program.
