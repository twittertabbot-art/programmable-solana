Launch rules

A report on one of the platform's own hooks, written from its code. It runs every coin of Module Mode.

The launcher picks the modules of a coin at launch. They are a sniper fee, a creator fee, rewards, a burn, a buy limit and a creator lock. A strict coin can also have a max wallet, an early lock and rewards without staking. No rate and no rule changes after launch.

Its code is programs/hook_rules/src/lib.rs in the package. RULES.md in the package describes every rule.

    What it does

    The launcher writes the coin's rules once, before the pool exists. On every buy and every sell the hook asks for one fee in the quote token. It is the sum of that side's creator share, rewards share and burn share. A trade later the hook splits what it collected into the creator's balance, the rewards and the burn pot.

    In the first minutes a buy can also pay a sniper fee, a higher pool fee that falls to the pool's own fee. A buy limit can cap one buy in that time. A creator lock can hold the launcher's first buy until a date.

    A coin with a strict module is a Token-2022 coin whose transfer hook is this program. The program then runs inside every transfer of the coin, for a max wallet, an early lock, the creator lock and rewards for registered holders.

    Who controls the program

    This is one of the platform's own hooks. Its upgrade authority is a key of Programmable that the platform whitelists, so the pool takes it without approval. Only a hook of the platform's own can be the transfer hook of a coin.

    Programmable's key can still upgrade it. An upgrade could change anything this report says. Programmable answers for it as it answers for the pool program itself.

    What it can take, and where it goes

    Each share is at most 5%, and the three shares of one side at most 10% together. The pool also cuts the sum to the platform's cap, now 10%. All shares are fixed at launch.

    The sniper fee starts at most at 50% and falls in a straight line to the pool's own fee within at most 10,800 slots, about 72 minutes. The pool cuts it to the override cap, now 50%. It stays in the pool's reserves, like any pool fee.

    The shares go to the hook's treasury for this coin, a token account of an address that only the hook signs for. The pool checks at creation that its hook treasury is this account. From there:

    • The creator share waits as the creator's balance. Only the creator claims it.
    • The rewards share goes to the wallets that stake the coin, or on a strict coin to the holders that registered. Each wallet claims only its own part. While less than 0.1% of the supply is staked or registered, this share goes to the burn pot.
    • The burn share collects in the burn pot. Anyone can spend the pot, only on a buy of the coin through the pool that burns what it buys. A run buys against a record of the pool at least 25 slots old and spends a slice of at most 0.5% of the pool's quote reserve.

    What can change after launch, and who changes it

    No rate, no window, no limit and no recipient changes after launch. No instruction rewrites the coin's rules, and the creator has no setter.

    InstructionWhoWhat it does
    claim_creatorthe creatorPays out the creator's balance, to an account the creator names.
    lock_creator, lock_creator_strictthe creatorLocks the creator's own coins until a date, at least the lock declared at launch and at most 365 days ahead. The date can only move later. On a strict coin the locked coins stay in the creator's wallet, which cannot sell or send them before the date.
    unlock_creator, unlock_creator_strictthe creator, after the dateFrees the locked coins.
    run_burn, run_burn_strictanyoneSpends a slice of the burn pot on a buy that burns the coin, within the limits above.
    sync_holderanyoneLowers a holder record to the wallet's real balance. It cannot raise it.
    stake, unstake, claim_rewardsthe wallet itselfStakes its own coins, takes them back about one hour after its last stake, and claims its own rewards.
    open_holder, claim_holder, close_holderthe wallet itselfRegisters the wallet for rewards on a strict coin, claims them, and closes its record for its rent.
    init_pool_config, init_strict_configthe launcher, onceWrite the coin's rules before the pool exists.
    init_manifestthe upgrade authority, onceWrites the manifest and the list of extra accounts.
    on_pool_create, before_swap, before_add_liquidity, executethe pool, or the token program in a transferThe calls above.

    A wallet's first stake opens a small record whose rent does not come back. A holder record gives its rent back when it is closed.

    Its manifest and its account lists

    init_manifest writes the manifest and the pool's list of extra accounts once. It needs the program's upgrade authority, and an account that exists cannot be created again. The list names one account, the coin's rules. A strict coin also has the list a token transfer reads, created once by init_strict_config. No instruction rewrites any of them.

    Programs it calls

    The token program, to move and burn coins. The pool program, for the buy of a burn. The system program and the associated token program, to create its accounts. Itself, to record each burn as an event. Inside a transfer of a strict coin it calls nothing.
