The collection

Buys of this coin earn pieces of your collection. Each piece is a real NFT in the buyer's wallet. Pieces stay tied to the coins: a wallet keeps one piece for every set amount of coins it holds, and a sell burns them.

Kind of coinfixed at launch

Works in every wallet and exchange, and can launch on the curve. Pick this one if you are not sure.

  • The coin is a normal Solana coin. Wallets, exchanges and other apps support it.
  • No program of ours runs when it moves between wallets. The hook never sees a sell.
  • A sell on this site burns the piece in the same transaction.
  • A plain send of coins leaves the piece behind. It burns a few seconds later.
  • A piece moves to another wallet only through Send on the coin page.

The pieces travel with the coins on every transfer. Fewer apps support it, and it starts with a seed you put in, not on the curve.

  • A transfer of the coin carries the piece to the receiver.
  • The receiver takes one piece for each whole unit of coins the transfer sends. A piece that cannot travel burns.
  • A sell burns the piece in the same trade.

What a pegged coin costs

  • Our program runs inside every transfer of the coin, sells included. On a plain coin the hook never sees a sell.
  • Fewer wallets, exchanges and apps support this kind of coin. A wallet app can fail to send it to a wallet that never held it.
  • A wallet needs a ticket to receive a piece. A ticket is a small account for this coin, and every wallet that bought here has one. A plain send to a wallet without one burns the piece. Send on the coin page opens the ticket first.
  • After a transfer made outside this site, the NFT picture in the wallet can follow a few seconds later.
  • The coin trades in this pool only.

Coins per piece: how many coins one piece needs. One buy earns a piece for each this many coins it buys. Coins from different buys do not add up.

Max supply: the most pieces that can exist at the same time. A burned piece frees its place for a later buy. Max pieces per buy: the most one single buy can earn, however big it is.

What these numbers mean

    When coins leave a wallet, it loses the pieces those coins backed, from the end of the holder's own list. Holders order their list on the coin page. A piece moves to another wallet only with its coins, so no marketplace can list it.
