Reading the hooks

Tax to treasury

A share of every buy and sell, in SOL, goes to a wallet you choose. The ceiling is fixed forever. You can lower or raise the taxes under it, or renounce and freeze them.

The tax collects as wrapped SOL in this wallet's wrapped SOL account. Its owner takes it from the coin page as plain SOL.

Launch guard

Buys pay a high fee in the first minutes. The fee falls every slot down to the pool fee and stays in the pool as liquidity. Nobody collects it, and nobody can add liquidity until the window is over. The hook is never called on a sell.

Your own first buy, if you make one, pays the start fee too.

Buyback and burn

A share of every trade, in SOL, collects in a treasury only the hook controls. Once it reaches the minimum, anyone can run a buyback. A run buys the coin with a slice of the treasury and burns what it buys. The first call records the pool's price, and a buy can follow 25 slots later.
